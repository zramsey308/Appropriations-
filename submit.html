FY'27 Community Project Funding (CPF) Requests

Submission Deadline: TBD
NOTE: Due to a limited number of requests allowed by each congressional office, the office of Congressman August Pfluger will only consider proposals that are:
  1. Submitted by organizations based in Congressional District TX-11; and
  2. Affect the constituents of Congressional District TX-11

Community Funding Projects MUST meet all guidelines established by the Committee:

  • Requestor MUST be able to demonstrate broad community support for the project.
  • Requestor MUST submit examples of community support to the office of Rep. August Pfluger in order for the request to be considered.
  • The project or requesting entity must have NO financial ties to the Member or his/her family.
  • Funding must be for FY'27 ONLY.
  • If the project would normally require a funding match or cost share by a non-federal entity, then the requesting entity will need to demonstrate that it can provide this match if it makes a CPF request.
  • The recipient of the CPF must be a governmental entity or a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Requests made by for-profit entities are NOT permitted.
